“Croatian isn’t only grammar and vocabulary. It’s the language of family lunches, phone calls with relatives and summers by the sea. I want you to be able to take part in all of it.”

Martin Stier

Bok! I’m Martin.

I’m Croatian and a Croatian language teacher. I recently moved to Melbourne with my wife, and I’ve been struck by how many people here have a Croatian story: a grandmother from Dalmatia, a partner from Zagreb, cousins in Slavonia they’d love to talk to properly.

My lessons are for exactly those people. I teach the Croatian that’s actually spoken today, with clear explanations of the grammar when you need it and plenty of speaking practice so it sticks.

How lessons work Kako funkcionira

Getting started is simple, and you don’t need to know your level.

  1. Send me a message

    Tell me a little about yourself, who the lessons are for and whether you’d prefer in person or online.

  2. We have a short chat

    I’ll get a sense of where you’re at, even if it’s just a few words you picked up from baka.

  3. I plan your lessons

    Conversation, grammar, reading, or games for kids. Lessons are built around what you want Croatian for.

  4. We start learning

    Regular lessons in Clifton Hill or online, at a pace that suits you.
